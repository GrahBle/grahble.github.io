Sesame Donuts Cost Analysis

Complete
Exceldata analysis
Problem
Store-level production and waste data existed, but no one had analyzed it closely enough to know whether the store was losing money to inefficiency.
Approach
Collected and analyzed production and waste values across the store to surface inefficiencies, then built a plan around what the numbers showed.
Result
Uncovered $40,000 in undocumented overages and presented the findings and a cost-control plan directly to the CEO, optimized for long-term operational efficiency.
Learned
How to turn raw operational data into a business case leadership could act on — not just a spreadsheet, but a recommendation.

College Scorecard Regression Analysis

Complete
RANOVAregression modeling
Problem
What factors actually predict a graduate's income ten years after leaving school, out of a large public dataset with many candidate variables?
Approach
Ran five-part statistical summaries to narrow the field of variables, applied both R-based and manual ANOVA computations to test for meaningful variance, then built a multivariable regression using matrix-based methods on the variables that held up.
Result
A regression model identifying the variables that were statistically significant predictors of post-graduation income.
Learned
How to move from a wide, messy set of candidate variables to a defensible model — and to check statistical work by hand as well as in code.
